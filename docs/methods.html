Methods & reproducibility

What each number on this site measures, how transition states are certified, and where every result comes from.

This page exists so that numbers on this site are never compared across estimators or reference states by accident. Every barrier quoted elsewhere names one of the estimators below and the reactant it is measured from.

Estimator glossary

Relaxed-scan maximum (constrained scan)
The highest point along a scan in which the two forming C–C distances are held equal at a sequence of values while every other coordinate relaxes; the barrier is measured from the first scan point. Cheap and robust, but the constrained path need not pass through the true saddle. Used for: raw and corrected Diels–Alder profiles (e.g. 36.05, 11.66, 9.87 kcal/mol).
CI-NEB highest image
The highest-energy physical image of a climbing-image nudged elastic band. An approximation to the saddle: it can carry a second imaginary mode, depends on the image count, and is only as good as the band’s convergence — which must be stated. Used for: raw MACE-OFF23, 37.20 kcal/mol (13 images; band convergence not recorded; one collapsed image excluded); 35.9 ± 1.0 over 11–21 images. A later standardized control recorded non-convergence, so NEB values here are diagnostics only.
Sella-refined saddle
A first-order saddle search (Sella, order = 1) started from a guess — an NEB image, a scan maximum, or another surface’s TS. Converged means the force criterion was met; it is not yet certified. Used for: raw MACE 36.2 (from the NEB image) and 35.81 (from the xTB TS).
Certified first-order saddle
A Sella-refined saddle that also passes every step of the transition-state standard below. Only these are called transition states on this site. Used for: corrected-model saddle 6.33 / 8.23 kcal/mol; iterations 0, 1, 2, 4.
Frozen-reactant reference
The barrier is measured from the model’s energy at the xTB-optimized reactant geometry, without relaxing it on the model being tested. Used for: 6.33 kcal/mol (corrected saddle); 35.81 kcal/mol (raw MACE saddle from the xTB TS).
Model-relaxed reactant reference
The reactant is first relaxed on the model being tested, so both ends of the barrier are stationary points of that model — the stricter internal-consistency check. Used for: 8.23 kcal/mol (same corrected saddle). A related variant relaxes the reactant with the forming bonds pinned, so the weakly bound complex cannot drift apart; used for the raw-MACE CI-NEB and its refined saddle.
Potential-energy barrier
A difference of potential energies between two structures on one surface (no entropy, no temperature). Every Diels–Alder barrier on this site is one.
Metadynamics free-energy surface
The negative of the accumulated bias from a metadynamics run, as a function of the biased collective variables. Its differences are free-energy differences at the simulation temperature, and its absolute levels are arbitrary offsets. Not comparable one-to-one with a potential-energy barrier. (The xTB RMSD-metadynamics runs in Studies 01–03 produce sampled potential-energy landscapes from a biased run, which are neither.)

What counts as a transition state

A structure is a certified first-order saddle only if all of the following hold:

  1. Sella saddle search with order = 1 converged to a maximum atomic force below 1e−4 eV/Å (Cartesian coordinates).
  2. Finite-difference Hessian at the converged structure: central differences with a 1e−3 Å displacement, symmetrized.
  3. Mass-weighted vibrational analysis: frequencies are the eigenvalues of M^−½ H M^−½ converted to wavenumbers (ν = 521.47·√λ cm⁻¹ for λ in eV Å⁻² amu⁻¹). Raw Hessian eigenvalues are never converted to frequencies.
  4. Exactly one significant imaginary mode: one frequency below −50 cm⁻¹.
  5. The imaginary mode is the reaction: forming C–C within 1.5–3.0 Å and an overlap of at least 0.30 between the mode’s Cartesian displacement and the forming-bond coordinate.

Matching a barrier number satisfies none of these. A scan maximum, NEB image, or uncertified stationary point is always labelled as such.

Known limitations of the standard On the xTB surface, identical structures intermittently show 1–3 imaginary modes because soft van der Waals modes of the reactant complex sit near the −50 cm⁻¹ cutoff; the handoff benchmark therefore reports reaching the reference saddle separately from strict Hessian cleanliness (Study 09). An early MACE saddle check (Study 04, Phase 1) counted imaginary modes without mass weighting; the count is valid, but its frequency value is not quoted anywhere.

Calculations

Reference — GFN2-xTB. xtb 6.7.1 for metadynamics, built-in path search and Hessians (frequencies read from the vibspectrum file, never parsed from standard output); xtb-python as an ASE calculator for scans, Sella, and force evaluations. Periodic runs use GFN1-xTB, which is a different level of theory.

Surrogates — MACE foundation models as ASE calculators:

  • MACE-OFF23 (small) for every Diels–Alder and water result; float64 for saddle searches and Hessians, float32 for MD.
  • MACE-OMOL-0 and MACE-POLAR-1 for the Sn-β work in progress, through a wrapper that always sets total charge and spin multiplicity explicitly.

Correction layer. Every correction is applied through a CorrectedCalculator that wraps the unmodified foundation model. The conservative pairwise Delta is a sum of Gaussian radial basis functions per element pair (8 functions × 6 pair channels = 48 coefficients, 3.6 Å cutoff), fit by ridge least squares (λ = 1e−3) on force residuals F_xTB − F_MACE; its forces are the analytic gradient of its energy, so it is conservative.

Data splits. Correction training and evaluation sets are split by parent path geometry (no leakage between rattled copies of one structure); the iterative loop keeps a fixed 48-configuration held-out reactive set that is never trained on.

Evidence standards

Statements on this site carry one of five labels:

  • Demonstrated directly measured in a result file of this repository.
  • Interpretation a supported explanation of a measured result — not itself demonstrated.
  • Open question not tested.
  • Work in progress infrastructure built; no scientific result yet.
  • Literature stated in, or read from a figure of, an external source — not measured here.

And four rules:

  1. Every number traces to a result file or a FINDINGS_*.md in the repository.
  2. xTB is the chosen reference, not ground truth; matching it is reference-matching.
  3. A superseded estimate is shown next to its replacement, never silently replaced.
  4. Negative results are reported plainly.
