Metadynamics of water: from clusters to a surrogate

Enhanced-sampling MD of water clusters with GFN2-xTB, and a MACE-OFF23 machine-learned potential as the route to accelerated sampling.

This is a running results notebook for the project. We validate GFN2-xTB RMSD-metadynamics workflows for water — bias and size sweeps, periodic and free droplets, and a Diels–Alder reaction — then benchmark a MACE-OFF23 foundation potential against xTB, with the longer-term aim of accelerating enhanced sampling.

NoteWhat the RMSD bias is

xTB biases along Cartesian RMSD to previously visited structures — not along any bond or angle. Every CV computed in analysis is a post-hoc descriptor, and Epot vs a CV is a sampled potential-energy landscape from a biased run, not a free-energy surface.

Results at a glance

# Study Headline result
01 Bias × size The bias breaks the weakest bond — H-bond for clusters, covalent O–H only for a lone molecule; small clusters evaporate.
02 PBC & temperature Periodicity confines the system (removes evaporation); a large O–O gap is drift, not dissociation.
03 Droplets Size suppresses evaporation — free n=20–50 droplets stay intact; raising kpush barely perturbs them (a bias-scaling artifact).
04 Diels–Alder A real reaction driven and verified: concerted TS, 6.7 kcal/mol barrier, −394 cm⁻¹ imaginary mode.
05 MACE-OFF23 MACE-OFF23 tracks xTB forces at r ≈ 0.99, reproduces droplet MD structure, and scales better (crossover by n≈50).
06 kpush scaling The RMSD-bias per-atom force ∝ kpush/N — so kpush must scale ∝ N; confirmed on the n=30 droplet.
07 CV design 7 candidate CVs collapse to ~3 axes (network integrity, size, chemistry); a minimal biasing set recommended.
08 Corrections A conservative delta correction removes ~all reaction-energy and ~2/3 barrier error MACE→xTB; scalar/element corrections don’t.

Methods in one paragraph

Gas-phase and periodic GFN2/GFN1-xTB metadynamics (built-in RMSD bias), NVT, 0.5 fs timestep; analysis with ASE / NumPy / pandas. Reactions via xtb --path + relaxed scan + Sella saddle optimization. Surrogate benchmarking with MACE-OFF23 (small) as an ASE calculator. All code, data and figures are in the repository; each study page links to its detailed FINDINGS document.
